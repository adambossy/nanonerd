Mastery Atlas

Large Language Models — a recursively generated curriculum, three levels deep. Every square is a leaf skill, colored by your latest quiz against its rubric.

–of the atlas colored green

The recursion

Each of the 16 areas fragments into its own curriculum, and each of those into leaf skills. Click a tile to zoom one level; press Esc to zoom back out.

The colors

Mastery is measured per leaf by a generated quiz with a rubric. Greens brighten as scores approach 100; yellow is partial; reds deepen with how badly a quiz went; blue is untouched territory.

The depth

Three levels are shown here. In the full tool the fragmentation continues — a mastered pixel at level 5 is invisible from the top, but its color still rolls up into the square above it.
