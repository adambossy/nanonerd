The recursion

Each of the 16 areas fragments into its own curriculum, and each of those into leaves. Click to zoom in — the tile magnifies in place; press Esc to zoom back out.

The colors

Mastery is measured per leaf by a generated quiz with a rubric. Greens brighten as scores approach 100; yellow is partial; reds deepen with how badly a quiz went; blue is untouched territory.

This variant

Blocks sit on a uniform grid, all the same size. Empty slots stay visible as faint dashed outlines — unfragmented capacity the curriculum could still grow into.
